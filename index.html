Avi

Avi is a private personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's permission, Avi can read the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar and draft replies, so it can summarise mail, prepare the day's agenda and help with routine work. Anything that sends, accepts or changes something always waits for the owner's explicit approval.
